The sweep changes S only, over a clipped 0.5× to 1.5× range. Payoff is max(S-K,0) for a call or max(K-S,0) for a put, with no premium subtraction. It is not profit, and the two curves are not at the same time. A model value need not exceed immediate payoff when rates or yield make early exercise valuable; this model cannot exercise early.

MODEL BOUNDARY

Useful structure.
Limited world.

Constant parameters, lognormal underlying dynamics, frictionless trading and continuous rebalancing. No jumps, stochastic volatility, funding spreads, fees, discrete dividends or early exercise. Actual market premiums are set by buyers and sellers.

All inputs are hypothetical. No security, broker or exchange is connected. No calibration, portfolio hedge, recommendation or forecast is produced. The price of an option can be lost in full.

NUMERICAL & PRIVACY NOTES

Local and explicit.

Seven days and 5% volatility are lower model limits; zero-time and zero-volatility singular Greeks are deliberately excluded. Year fraction is days/365, with no exchange calendar or day-count inference. Values below numerical or display precision may round to zero.

Inputs stay in page memory. No storage, telemetry, requests or exports. Reset restores the illustrative defaults. Original implementation and charts, no external runtime assets.
